## Accuracy — read this part

GPS speed readings depend entirely on your device's hardware and on satellite
signal conditions at the time. Readings can be wrong, and are reliably poor
indoors, inside vehicles, in tunnels, in built-up areas, under dense tree cover,
and at walking pace.

**The site makes no guarantee of accuracy.** The accuracy figure shown on screen
is your device's own estimate, not a certification.
