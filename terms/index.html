What the service is

Self Speed is a free, browser-based speedometer that reads your device's GPS. It is provided as a convenience tool, at no cost, with no account and no warranty.

Accuracy — read this part

GPS speed readings depend entirely on your device's hardware and on satellite signal conditions at the time. Readings can be wrong, and are reliably poor indoors, inside vehicles, in tunnels, in built-up areas, under dense tree cover, and at walking pace.

The site makes no guarantee of accuracy. The accuracy figure shown on screen is your device's own estimate, not a certification.
